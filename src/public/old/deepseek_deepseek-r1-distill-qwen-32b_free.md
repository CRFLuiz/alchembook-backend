Creating two e-books on surfing, one for fans and another for practicing surfers, involves a comprehensive approach to cover all essential aspects. Here's an organized outline based on the thought process:

### E-book 1: "Surfing: A Journey Through Waves and Passion" (For Fans)

**Chapter 1: Introduction to Surfing Culture**
- **Historical Overview**: Origins of surfing in ancient Polynesia, its cultural significance in Hawaii, and its evolution into a global sport.
- **What is Surfing?**: Basic definition and its appeal.

**Chapter 2: The Surfer’s Gear**
- **Boards**: Types and evolution.
- **Fins, Leashes, Wetsuits**: Function and importance.
- **Accessories**: Impact on performance.

**Chapter 3: Surfing Terminology**
- **Common Terms**: Wipe out, hang ten, etc.
- **Wave Types**: Tubes, peaks, shoulders.
- **Surf Breaks**: Characteristics and significance.

**Chapter 4: Surfing Techniques and Styles**
- **Basic Techniques**: Popping up, stance, riding, turning.
- **Advanced Techniques**: Cutbacks, aerials.
- **Styles**: Longboarding, shortboarding, tow-in.

**Chapter 5: Surfing Competitions**
- **Competition Structure**: Point systems, heats, judging.
- **Major Events**: World Surf League, others.
- **Qualification and Scoring**: Understanding the process.

**Chapter 6: Brazilian National Athletes**
- **Prominent Surfers**: Achievements and contributions.
- **Their Stories**: Background, challenges, impact.

**Chapter 7: The Future of Surfing**
- **Environmental Issues**: Sustainability efforts.
- **Technological Innovations**: Equipment evolution.
- **Cultural Impact**: Mainstream influence and media.

### E-book 2: "Mastering the Waves: Tips and Maneuvers" (For Surfers)

**Chapter 1: Essential Techniques for Surfers**
- **Basics**: Popping up, stance, balance, riding.
- **Intermediate Techniques**: Turning, carving.
- **Wave Reading and Paddling**: Strategies and tips.

**Chapter 2: Safety and Etiquette**
- **Rules of the Wave**: Right of way, avoiding collisions.
- **Safety Tips**: For various wave sizes and conditions.
- **Environmental Respect**: Etiquette and first aid basics.

**Chapter 3: Advanced Maneuvers**
- **Complex Moves**: Aerials, spins, radical turns.
- **Guidance**: Preparation, step-by-step instructions.

**Chapter 4: Surfing in Different Conditions**
- **Technique and Strategy**: Adjusting to wave sizes.
- **Equipment Choices**: For different scenarios.
- **Weather and Tides**: Reading forecasts and patterns.

**Chapter 5: Training and Improvement**
- **Physical Training**: Strength, flexibility, endurance.
- **Mental Training**: Goal setting, progress tracking.
- **Coaching and Motivation**: Finding a mentor, staying inspired.

This structured approach ensures each e-book is comprehensive, engaging, and informative, catering to the specific needs of fans and practicing surfers alike.
